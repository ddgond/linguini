I want Linguini to be a Moonlight client (built on top of an existing one) that takes inspiration by the great deeds of the mighty Tortellini, the fish who conquered Elden Ring. A core requirement is that it runs cross-platform.

Rather than just rendering the game stream, it needs to render a full 3D environment, designed to be a streamer's bedroom/office. In it, is a camera pointed at a fish tank, and the player is the fish. They control the fish via Real Fishy Movement TM, and can swim to one side of the tank and look at the computer monitor in the room to watch the game stream. Inside the tank are flash cards with controller prompts on them, and swimming in front of them triggers the button press.

Real Fishy Movement TM is a goofy name for what is essentially a fish-simulator-controller. Rather than precise movement controls, it should simplify the interface of controlling a fish so that movement can look like that of a real fish. Third-person camera is a must. Pressing no inputs should drift slowly the way a real fish does when idle.

Finally, the client should also be able to stream the in-office camera's perspective, say, to Twitch or OBS, as if running a real ML-based fish tracking application in a livestream.
