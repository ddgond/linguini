Linguini: a Moonlight client where you are the fish.

Requirements
  - macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel
  - A host PC running Sunshine or GeForce Experience with GameStream

Install
  1. Unzip and move Linguini.app to Applications.
  2. Open it. Linguini is ad-hoc signed but not notarized by Apple, so the
     first launch is blocked. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, choose
     "Open Anyway" for Linguini, then confirm Open.
     Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
  3. When macOS asks whether Linguini may find devices on your local network,
     allow it; that's how it reaches your host.

Pairing and settings are stored in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Linguini/
